# Methods and limitations: reading notes for the research report

1. **Scale:** The NASA POWER coordinate is regional climate forcing for Hex River Valley, not a vineyard station. The Dagbreek polygon is the satellite block-level area (~3.24 ha). The MERRA-2 source elevation is grid-region average.
2. **Sentinel:** COPERNICUS/S2_SR_HARMONIZED; 30% scene-level metadata cloud threshold; pixel-level SCL mask of 3,8,9,10,11; reflectance scaling 0.0001; B4,B5,B8,B11; NDVI/EVI2/NDRE/NDMI. Mean over polygon at 20 m, average multiple scenes on a date. Earth Engine end date **exclusive**. The 2017–2024 extraction must be finalized on one source (currently archived 219 dates; another run had 223).
3. **Canopy proxy:** The scaled block-mean NDVI is a **vegetation-cover/canopy development proxy**, not field-measured vine fractional canopy cover. Pooling NDVI endmembers (5th/95th) across seasons ensures a common empirical scale. Inter-row cover, soil and shadows can alter NDVI.
4. **Smoothing:** Time-interpolate preserving all original dates to a 5-day grid, then a seven-point second-order SG filter (nominal ~35 days). Sparse periods and long gaps create uncertainty; original observations must be shown beside the reconstruction.
5. **Phenology:** Budbreak/green-up (0.05 with sustained positive trend), full budbreak (0.10 sustained), first maximum, post-peak persistent decline and 90% peak threshold, optional late-season 0.50 threshold. These are *operational land-surface phenological indicators*. They are **not exact vine BBCH stage observations**. Late season threshold is not physiological maturity.
6. **GDD:** GDD method 1 with base 10°C and upper 35°C; clip the daily mean temperature before subtracting the base. Budbreak is the zero-reference label; the start day's GDD is included in the cumulative sequence for later event dates. Source of upper temperature threshold remains to be checked against primary paper.
7. **Grape parameters:** Literature-motivated custom Crop; all parameters and uncertain provenance recorded in `config/parameter_provenance.csv`. `PlantPop`/`SeedSize` numerically obtain CC0=.064, not vineyard planting density. `Kcb` explicitly set to 1.10 as a research intended value, subject to paper verification. An emergence floor of 10 GDD is a numerical fix, not biology. Informed CGC is inverted to reproduce Sentinel peak timing; this is an *in-sample constraint*, not independent validation.
8. **Simulation:** Same satellite-based budbreak date used in both configurations. Simulations started on 1 April, initial soil water field capacity on that date. SandyLoam built-in soil and well-watered SMT=100 are assumptions, not farm survey data. Technical maturity is a model execution workaround to keep the crop active to 30 April and is not grape physiological maturity. AquaCrop-OSPy is a limited approximation of perennial grapevines.
9. **Evaluation:** Raw-scale model CC vs NDVI-scaled proxy has a scale mismatch; normalized comparisons remove maximum-amplitude differences and assess timing/shape. R² can be negative. Metrics are evaluated against satellite data that are also used to set informed crop parameters, so they are not independent field validation. For a sensitivity check, compute errors on direct acquisition dates in addition to smoothed dates.
10. **Water:** Irrigation, Tr, Es, ETa, drainage and runoff are model simulation outputs, not measured farm use, field-calibrated ET, or savings. `ETa=Tr+Es`; daily outputs must cover matching budbreak–30 April dates. The Kcb correction means historical water figures must be rerun.
11. **Conclusion:** Feasibility of satellite-informed phenological parameterisation may be supported, but equivalence to field phenology, independent AquaCrop validation, yields and actual irrigation savings are not established.
